**Porpoise 2.1.1** is a polish update.

- **Achievements lists:** a new Achievements tab in the in-game menu, and each game's list from its Details (Square), with badges.
- **Widescreen per game:** over 300 GameCube games now play in true 16:9 by default, with no pop-in at the edges. Widescreen codes by **Warped Polygon**.
- Faster start on consoles where no jailbreak answers.
- Optimizations and fixes for reported bugs.

Help and bug reports: **[RIPALDA Discord](https://discord.gg/GgDE5Vynyu)**.

## Install or update

**From 2.0 or later:** **Settings → About → Updates**.

**By hand:** download **`Porpoise-2.1.1.zip`**, delete `/data/homebrew/PPSA99764/` on the PS5, and copy the **`PPSA99764`** folder into **`/data/homebrew/`**. Your games, saves and settings in `/data/porpoise/` are kept.

Porpoise contains no games, BIOS or firmware. Play only games you own. Not affiliated with Nintendo, Sony or RetroAchievements. Licenses are in `licenses/README.txt` inside the zip.
